## Where OT puts the burden

<Todo>

Transform incoming operations against unseen concurrent ones. Note the consequence you care about: a consistent transformation order is required, which in practice means a central authority. Worth stating what that buys — compact messages, no tombstones.

</Todo>

## Where CRDTs put the burden

<Todo>

Commutative updates: order-independent by construction, so no authority is needed. The cost is per-element metadata that outlives the content. If you can, include a concrete measurement from Conflux of how much metadata a realistic session accumulates — that number would make this post worth reading on its own.

</Todo>

## What convergence does not give you

<Todo>

The point most write-ups skip and the one worth making: convergence guarantees replicas agree, not that the merged result is what any author intended. A worked example of two concurrent edits producing a valid-but-nonsense merge would land this well.

</Todo>
